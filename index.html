Ian Persaud

I'm a manager of products, programs, and projects in the tech space with a preference for solving hard problems in healthcare. I'm also a business owner, an artist/musician, a writer, and a big reader.

When

I've been a product & project manager since 2021, I've been writing music since 2015, and I've been worried about healthcare since 2010. I've been disillusioned by the speed of policy change since 2014, and I've been determined that tech could meet people's needs faster than policy since 2015.

Where

I'm based in Queens, NY. Born and raised, baby!

Why

I enjoy thinking hard — it's something I took for granted back in school. I also care a lot about my fellow humans, and I want better for all of us.

I want to build things that remain in my absence, enriching the community — big or small — after I'm gone. So I build healthcare products, run projects, and make art. My business exists to help novice and amateur musicians make their first records, because that first record matters, even if there is no second.

That which can be destroyed by the truth should be.
