My Room Is a Mess EP

I spent 1 week in the autumn at Stone Mountain Farms for my birthday, alone in a cabin with no wifi. Somewhere between camping and glamping. I brought the studio with me, and gave myself the gift of unpressured time and solitude to fill with ideas. Sonic isolation and a real experience of being alone — something that's very rare for me since I've lived in cities my whole life.
