It's nice to meet you

I've been meaning to build a website for a long time. The blank canvas was intimidating, but I'm glad to finally get the brush moving.

Perhaps there's something about turning 30, but I've found a deep reinvigoration of my personal ambition to learn, and execute. I had gotten so deep in my thinking and learning that I had not executed as fully, but today I am so much more focused on the doing.

The website will evolve and iterate rapidly at first, and I wonder if it will ever reach a stable format... but I'm not going to think about that just yet; I'm going to just do.
